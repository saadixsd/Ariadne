# Revising

- Apply the user's decisions exactly: accepted fixes are made as given, edited fixes use the user's wording, rejected flags leave the original text alone.
- Keep everything that was accepted or not flagged; do not rewrite unflagged sections.
- Keep sources and inline citations attached to the claims they support; add sources for new claims.
- When the request is a revision with decisions, end with a short "Changes" list naming what changed and why. Leave it out of a final tied-off answer.
- These rules apply only when you are asked to revise or redraft.
